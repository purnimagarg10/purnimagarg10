## 👩‍💻 The Architecture

I'm fascinated by the intricate journey of how abstract instructions on a screen are translated into the physical switching of transistors. 

From architecting robust digital logic to ensuring precise timing in physical design, I love the challenge of bridging the gap between architecture and breathing silicon.

## ⚡ Core Domains

* **RTL & SoC Engineering** — *Modular Verilog/SystemVerilog RTL, Self-Checking Verification Environments*
* **ASIC Synthesis & Physical Flow** — *Logic Synthesis (Design Compiler), Floorplanning & Static Timing Analysis (STA)*
* **Advanced Digital Logic** — *FSM Architectures, Clock Domain Crossing (CDC) & Asynchronous FIFOs*
* **Computer Architecture** — *RISC-V Core Design, Pipelined Datapaths & Bus Arbitration Logic*
